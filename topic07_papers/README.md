# Papers

There will (probably) be no more programming assignments in this class.

We will focus on reading papers for the rest of the semester.

1. This will give you time to work on your project.

1. For each paper below, read it before the designated class.

1. You should spend at least 3 hours reading the paper.

    1. Many of the papers will be quite hard and technical.

    1. I don't expect you to understand everything.

    1. But I do expect you to make some effort to work through difficulties you encounter (e.g. using Google/ChatGPT).

    1. You should be reading in "Interpreter mode" not "Transformer mode".  (That is, you're brain will need to do "real computation" to understand the material.)

1. Classes won't be recorded.

    So please be present / on time.
